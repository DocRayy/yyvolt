# V2-5c: Baku
Layout order (stylized): very long waterfront start/finish straight, a tight right 90-degree
Turn 1, Turns 2 and 3 (left, left), more 90-degree corners through modern city blocks,
a tight left around the old city wall, a very narrow old-town section with a chicane
(still at least 3 x CAR_WIDTH wide), then fast sweepers and a late left kink back onto the
long straight with the sea on one side.
Visuals: glass towers, old-town sandstone walls with battlements (instanced boxes), a hill
with three curved flame-shaped towers in the distance (extruded shapes, animated emissive
at night), the Caspian sea plane with simple waves, palms and boulevard lamps, grandstands,
visible barriers. Day: bright dry sun with light haze. Night: city lights and the glowing
towers, restrained bloom.
