# V2-5a: Monaco
Layout order, clockwise from the start/finish straight: Sainte Devote (right 90 degrees),
climb uphill, Massenet (left), Casino Square (right then left), Mirabeau (right), the Grand
Hotel hairpin (very tight left), Portier (right), a straight tunnel, the harbor chicane
(left-right), Tabac (right), the swimming pool chicanes (left-right, right-left), La Rascasse
(right), Antony Noghes (right), back to the start/finish straight. Mild hill, mostly flat.
Visuals: harbor water on one side with yachts (simple hulls), pastel apartment blocks and
hotels, a casino building, a covered tunnel with lights, hillside with pines, red-white kerbs,
visible metal guardrails along both sides. Day: warm Mediterranean sun, clear sky.
Night: warm street lamps, lit windows, yacht lights.
